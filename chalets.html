Eland Chalet

Your private slice of Karoo silence

Where the horizon is your only neighbour and the stars are your evening entertainment. Eland Chalet is a modern-meets-rustic hideaway designed for guests who know that the best luxury is found in the stillness of the wild.

1 BedroomDouble BedBathroomBraaiKitchenFireplaceWildlife Views

Inside, the charm of the Karoo comes to life. Sink into the lounge as the fireplace crackles, prepare easy self-catering meals in the equipped kitchen, then retire to crisp percale linen after a day of exploring.

Step out onto your spacious undercover veranda to braai beneath the vast African sky or simply watch wildlife drift across the plains. Powered by the sun and the silence, this is a place to unplug, breathe and reset.

Gemsbok Chalet

Spacious thatched comfort for family stays

Gemsbok Chalet forms part of our family collection: a warm, thatched retreat designed for connection, comfort and mountain-view mornings in the Karoo.

2 BedroomsDouble & SinglesFamily FlexibleBathroomGas StoveBraaiFireplace

The chalet features a welcoming lounge with an indoor fireplace, a self-catering kitchen equipped for family meals, and flexible sleeping options for adults and children. A cot or extra bed can be arranged on request.

After a day on the reserve, wash off the dust of the trail, light the braai on your covered porch and settle into a front-row seat to mountain sunsets and passing wildlife.
