Eland Chalet

Your private slice of Karoo silence

Eland Chalet is tucked into a quieter part of the reserve, ideal for couples or small families who want a little more privacy. It includes a comfortable lounge with fireplace, a dining area, self-catering kitchen, undercover braai and a spacious veranda for slow mornings and star-filled evenings.

1 BedroomDouble BedBathroomBraaiKitchenFireplaceWildlife Views

Simple, warm and practical, this chalet gives visitors a clear base from which to explore the reserve and return to quiet comfort.

Gemsbok Chalet

Where tradition meets the untamed

Gemsbok Chalet carries the classic thatched Karoo character visitors expect from a mountain reserve stay. It offers an easy self-catering layout, a fireplace for cooler nights, undercover braai facilities and an outdoor porch for taking in the open views.

1 BedroomDouble BedBath & ShowerGas StoveBraaiMountain Views

A good fit for couples, parents with a young child, or anyone who wants the charm of a traditional chalet without feeling far from the lodge experience.
