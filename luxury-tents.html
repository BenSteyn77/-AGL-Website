The Luxury Tent Experience

Unplug, breathe and rediscover the wild

The tents sit about 2km from the main lodge, where wildlife, birdsong and quiet views shape the rhythm of the day.

1 BedroomQueen BedBathroomSelf-CateringBoma-BraaiSolar LightsWildlife Views

Comfort under canvas

Inside, the tent offers a queen-size bed dressed in crisp linen, comfortable tent furniture and solar lighting for reading at night. The stoep outside has wooden furniture for taking in the scenery, and the private boma-braai creates the perfect place to cook, gather and watch the evening settle.

Attached to the tent is a bathroom with flush toilet and bath. The tent includes basic self-catering amenities, a 12-volt fridge/freezer and gas stove. Guests can feel properly immersed in nature while still having the essentials close at hand.

Children are welcome at the lodge. For families considering the tents, please contact us if you would like guidance on the best fit for your group.
